How it works

Talk. Let go. It's written.

Cado sits in the menu bar and stays out of the way until you ask for it.

  1. 01

    Hold to talk

    Hold ⌃⇧ in any app. Cado listens while you speak.

  2. 02

    Let go

    Your speech is transcribed on your Mac, usually in about a second.

  3. 03

    Text lands

    Words appear at the cursor, with punctuation, and your clipboard is put back.

Dictation

Type with your voice, anywhere you can type.

Browsers, native apps, the Terminal and the Claude Code CLI. Press ⌃⌥Space to start and again to stop for longer passages. Esc cancels.

  • Hold-to-talkHold, speak, release.
  • Toggle modeHands free for long thoughts.
  • Your modelPicked for your chip and RAM.

Meetings

Notes that say who said what.

Cado records your mic as Me and only the meeting app, such as Zoom, Teams or Meet in a browser, as Others. Stop, and a Markdown note lands in your notes folder.

  • Ask firstCado offers to record when a meeting starts. It never starts by itself.
  • Summaries Coming soonKey points, decisions and action items, written on your Mac.
  • Your folderPoint it at an Obsidian vault and notes appear there.

Private by design

Your voice stays on your Mac.

No account and no tracking. Here is exactly what leaves the machine, which is almost nothing.

Never leaves your Mac

  • Your audioMeeting audio is held in memory and discarded once the note is written.
  • What you say, as textTranscripts go to your notes folder and nowhere else.
  • Your settings

What does use the network

  • Model downloadsFrom huggingface.co, only when you click Download. Files are checked against a pinned checksum.
  • Update checkOn GitHub, about once a day. Turn it off in Settings.
  • Anonymous launch countOff by default. Never audio, text or file names.
